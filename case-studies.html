Automotive & Mobility

AI Service Desk for a Tier-1 Automotive Supplier

Challenge

A Tier-1 automotive supplier's IT service desk was handling rising ticket volumes across engineering, plant and corporate IT, with response times slipping against contracted SLAs.

Solution

Deployed an IT Service Desk AI agent trained on the existing known-error database, integrated with the ticketing system to triage, resolve known issues directly, and route the rest with full context attached. Paired with an Incident Management redesign for P1/P2 escalation.

Results

Within one quarter, routine requests were resolved without human involvement and major incident communication became automatic, freeing the service desk team to focus on complex, plant-critical issues.

46%Faster P1 resolution
31%Tickets deflected
3 weeksTo first pilot live
Financial Services

Invoice Automation for a Multi-Entity Finance Team

Challenge

A financial services group was processing supplier invoices manually across five European entities, each with different approval chains and a growing backlog of exceptions.

Solution

Built a Finance AI workflow combining Document AI extraction, three-way matching against purchase orders, and entity-specific approval routing, with a Workflow Automation layer handling exceptions and audit logging.

Results

Manual data entry was removed from the majority of invoices, approval cycle time dropped, and finance leadership gained a real-time view of processing status across all five entities.

€2.1MProcessed monthly
68%Manual touch removed
5Entities covered
Human Resources

Employee Onboarding for a Scaling Tech Company

Challenge

A fast-growing technology company was onboarding dozens of new hires a month through a 14-step manual checklist spread across HR, IT and facilities, with frequent delays to day-one readiness.

Solution

Deployed an HR AI agent to run the onboarding workflow end-to-end: triggering account provisioning, equipment orders and a day-one checklist automatically once an offer was accepted, with HR retaining approval on any policy exception.

Results

New hires now arrive to fully provisioned accounts and equipment on day one as standard, and HR spends its time on the parts of onboarding that need a human, not the checklist.

9 daysReduced to 1 day
100%Day-one readiness
0Manual tickets for standard onboarding
