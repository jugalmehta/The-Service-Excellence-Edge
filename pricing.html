Questions

Frequently asked

What's included in the Automation Starter package?

One fully scoped workflow or chatbot automation, up to two system integrations, and a monthly performance report — ideal for proving value on a single, well-defined process.

How is the Enterprise Transformation tier scoped?

We run a short discovery phase to map your current service operation against the capability matrix, then propose a fixed-fee or milestone-based programme sized to what's actually in scope.

Can we move between tiers?

Yes. Most engagements start with Automation Starter or the AI Automation Package and expand into Enterprise Transformation once the first automation is proven.

Do prices include ongoing support?

Automation Starter includes email support; the AI Automation Package and Enterprise Transformation include priority support and a recurring optimisation review.

Is our data used to train external models?

No. Agents run against your systems and approved knowledge sources under your data residency requirements; nothing is used to train third-party models.

Still deciding which package fits?

Tell us what you're automating and we'll recommend a starting point.
