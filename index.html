Questions

Before you reach out

Do we need to rip out our existing tools?
No — automations and AI agents connect into what you already use (ticketing, CRM, HRIS, finance tools). Nothing new to roll out company-wide.
What's the difference between the AI automation work and the ITSM consulting?
Automation ships working AI chatbots, workflows, and support agents. ITSM consulting maps your processes, tracks your SLAs, and gives you the risk and maturity picture behind them. Most clients start with one and add the other once they see where the gaps are.
How long does a first engagement take?
A first automation — like a chatbot or one workflow — typically ships in weeks, not quarters. ITSM deliverables like a process map or maturity report run on their own timeline once we have access to your ticket and workflow data.
Is our data safe?
We work within your existing systems and access controls. Nothing about your operation leaves your infrastructure without your sign-off.
Who will I actually be working with?
Directly with the person doing the work — no account manager layer, no handoffs to someone new mid-project.
