AI Agents

Five agents, each scoped to one job

We don't deploy a single general-purpose bot. Each agent has its own scope, guardrails, escalation path and success metric.

AI Agent

Customer Support AI

Handles first-line customer enquiries across chat and email, drafting or sending responses within approved boundaries.

  • Answers from your approved knowledge base
  • Drafts replies for review or sends autonomously
  • Escalates on low confidence or sentiment risk
  • Reports deflection rate and CSAT impact
In practice: Deflects routine order-status and account questions, leaving agents for complex cases.
AI Agent

HR AI

Answers policy questions and runs structured HR workflows like onboarding and leave requests.

  • Policy Q&A grounded in your HR documentation
  • Runs onboarding checklists automatically
  • Handles leave and benefits requests
  • Keeps HR data changes within approval rules
In practice: Guides a new hire through their first-week checklist without HR opening a ticket.

Finance AI

Processes invoices, flags anomalies and prepares items for human approval.

  • Three-way matching against POs and receipts
  • Anomaly and duplicate detection
  • Approval routing by amount and entity
  • Full audit trail for finance controls
In practice: Matches and routes 80% of incoming supplier invoices without manual entry.

IT Service Desk AI

  • Automatic ticket classification and priority
  • Resolves against a known-error database

  • Tracks resolution time against SLA
